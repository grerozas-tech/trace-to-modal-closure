# Post-confirmatory robustness outputs

These CSVs were generated during adversarial pre-release review and are explicitly exploratory/robustness analyses. They do not replace or redefine the prospectively frozen primary tests in `results/main/`.

- `fixed_dynamic_renorm_robustness_64.csv` — G01 reference-reproduction audit with and without explicit post-closure norm restoration.
- `dynamic_closure_multicontext_robustness_64x4.csv` — four symmetry-related calibration-to-test pairs over the 64-seed C07 cohort (256 seed-pair cells).
- `operator_epsilon_robustness_16.csv` — C07 finite-difference sensitivity over 16 seeds.
